## Provenance

The shipped lessons are **generated by a large language model and QA-reviewed
by the pipeline in this repository**. They contain no verbatim sentences copied
from a third-party corpus: every exported lesson carries
`grounding_mode: "fallback_no_wiki"` and no `source_id`/`origin` provenance
fields.
