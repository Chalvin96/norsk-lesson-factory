The [shared glossary](../../content/terminology/glossary.yaml) is the canonical record
of these labels; this page explains the learner-load rationale.

Terminology guidance exists to reduce learner load, not to prescribe exhaustive
language. Introduce technical terms only when needed, explain them plainly, and add a
Norwegian label where it helps.

Use preferred labels consistently and keep early titles and labels concise; consistency
prevents synonym drift. A small shared glossary records these choices, while authors and
reviewers retain judgment for context.
