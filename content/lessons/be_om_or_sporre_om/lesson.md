## At the hotel reception {#at-the-hotel-reception role=orient objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Nora is staying at a small hotel in Bergen. She needs practical help, but she also needs information. At reception, she speaks to Eirik.

::: {.reading translation="Hi. I’m staying here for two nights." dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Hei. Jeg skal bo her i to netter.
:::

::: {.reading translation="Welcome! Is there anything you need?" dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Velkommen! Er det noe du trenger?
:::

::: {.reading translation="Yes, first I would like to ask about breakfast. When does it start?" dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Ja, først vil jeg gjerne spørre om frokosten. Når begynner den?
:::

::: {.reading translation="Breakfast starts at half past six." dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Frokosten begynner klokka halv sju.
:::

::: {.reading translation="Thank you. May I also ask for an extra towel?" dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Takk. Kan jeg også be om et ekstra håndkle?
:::

::: {.reading translation="Of course. I’ll send a towel up to your room." dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Selvfølgelig. Jeg sender et håndkle opp til rommet ditt.
:::

::: {.reading translation="I have to leave early tomorrow. May I ask about the price of a taxi to the airport?" dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Jeg må dra tidlig i morgen. Kan jeg spørre om prisen på en taxi til flyplassen?
:::

::: {.reading translation="It usually costs around five hundred kroner." dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Det koster vanligvis rundt fem hundre kroner.
:::

::: {.reading translation="That’s fine. May I ask you to book a taxi for seven o’clock?" dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Det går fint. Kan jeg be deg om å bestille en taxi til klokka sju?
:::

::: {.reading translation="Yes, I’ll take care of that." dialogue_id=at-hotel-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Ja, det ordner jeg.
:::

Nora uses two expressions that English often translates with ask:

::: examples
- no: be om
- en: ask for; request
- no: spørre om
- en: ask about; ask for information about
:::

The difference depends on the result Nora wants. She uses spørre om when she wants to know something. She uses be om when she wants to receive an item or wants someone to do something.

## First checkpoint: identify the intended result {#first-checkpoint-intended-result role=contrast objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Return to Nora’s conversation. For each use of be om or spørre om, state what Nora expects to receive: information, an item, or an action.

This activity belongs here because the intended result is the central distinction. Use the hotel context rather than translating ask mechanically. A successful response will correctly identify breakfast times and taxi prices as information, the towel as an item, and booking the taxi as an action.

## Asking for an item or action with be om {#asking-for-an-item-or-action role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

The infinitive is å be, and the present-tense form is ber.

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber om et glass vann.
- en: I ask for a glass of water.
- no: Hun ber om regningen.
- en: She asks for the bill.
- no: Vi ber om to ekstra stoler.
- en: We ask for two extra chairs.
:::

In these examples, the speaker wants someone to provide an item. After om, the item is named directly:

::: examples
- no: be om + et glass vann
- en: ask for + a glass of water
- no: be om + regningen
- en: ask for + the bill
- no: be om + to ekstra stoler
- en: ask for + two extra chairs
:::

Be om is also used when you request help or a service.

::: examples
- no: Han ber om hjelp.
- en: He asks for help.
- no: De ber om rask service.
- en: They request quick service.
:::

To request a specific action from a person, a common pattern is:

::: examples
- no: be + person + om å + action
- en: ask + person + to + action
:::

Nora says:

::: examples
- no: Kan jeg be deg om å bestille en taxi?
- en: May I ask you to book a taxi?
:::

The person comes before om, and the requested action follows om å.

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber naboen om å lukke vinduet.
- en: I ask the neighbor to close the window.
- no: Hun ber kollegaen om å sende dokumentet.
- en: She asks her colleague to send the document.
- no: Vi ber sjåføren om å vente i fem minutter.
- en: We ask the driver to wait for five minutes.
:::

Polite questions with kan are especially useful:

::: examples
- no: Kan jeg be om en kopp kaffe?
- en: May I ask for a cup of coffee?
- no: Kan jeg be deg om å snakke litt saktere?
- en: May I ask you to speak a little more slowly?
:::

In both cases, the speaker wants a practical result. The coffee should be provided; the other person should change how they speak.

## Controlled practice: requesting things and actions {#controlled-practice-requesting-things-and-actions role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Write one Norwegian request for each situation:

- At a café, request the bill.
- At a hotel, request another pillow.
- Ask a colleague to send an email.
- Ask a taxi driver to wait outside.
- At an office, request a copy of a document.

This activity comes after the basic pattern because it keeps the meaning constant: every situation requires an item or action. Use be om throughout, adding a person and om å when someone must perform a specific action. A successful response will name the requested item directly after om or place the requested person before om å.

## Asking for information with spørre om {#asking-for-information role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

The infinitive is å spørre, and the present-tense form is spør.

Use spørre om when the purpose is to obtain information.

::: examples
- no: Jeg spør om prisen.
- en: I ask about the price.
- no: Hun spør om åpningstidene.
- en: She asks about the opening hours.
- no: Vi spør resepsjonisten om frokosten.
- en: We ask the receptionist about breakfast.
- no: De spør læreren om prøven.
- en: They ask the teacher about the test.
:::

The words after om identify the topic. The speaker expects an explanation or answer, not the object itself.

Consider Nora’s first question:

::: examples
- no: Jeg vil gjerne spørre om frokosten. Når begynner den?
- en: I would like to ask about breakfast. When does it start?
:::

Frokosten is the topic. The following question shows what information Nora needs.

You can also include the person who has the information:

::: examples
- no: Nora spør Eirik om taxiprisen.
- en: Nora asks Eirik about the taxi price.
- no: Jeg spør servitøren om dagens suppe.
- en: I ask the waiter about today’s soup.
- no: Amir spør legen om medisinen.
- en: Amir asks the doctor about the medicine.
:::

These sentences do not request the taxi price, soup, or medicine as objects to be handed over. They introduce topics about which the speaker wants information.

## Second checkpoint: formulate information questions {#second-checkpoint-formulate-information-questions role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

For each context, write only the first sentence of a two-sentence exchange: introduce the topic with spørre om. Use the supplied direct question unchanged as the second sentence.

Use these contexts and supplied questions:

- breakfast at a hotel — Når begynner frokosten? / When does breakfast start?
- the price of a train ticket — Hvor mye koster en togbillett? / How much does a train ticket cost?
- opening hours at a museum — Når åpner museet? / When does the museum open?
- a course at a language school — Når begynner kurset? / When does the course start?

The supplied question makes the precise information need clear. This activity scores only the choice and formation of the spørre om topic introduction, not direct-question construction. It keeps the practice focused on topic framing.

## Compare the two expressions {#compare-the-two-expressions role=contrast objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

The same setting can require either expression. Focus on what the speaker wants to happen.

**At a restaurant**

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber om menyen.
- en: I ask for the menu.
:::

The speaker wants the physical menu.

::: examples
- no: Jeg spør om menyen.
- en: I ask about the menu.
:::

The speaker wants information about the menu, perhaps whether vegetarian dishes are available.

**At a hotel**

::: examples
- no: Hun ber om frokost på rommet.
- en: She requests breakfast in her room.
:::

She wants a service.

::: examples
- no: Hun spør om frokosten.
- en: She asks about breakfast.
:::

She wants information, perhaps its time or location.

**At a station**

::: examples
- no: Han ber den ansatte om å bestille en billett.
- en: He asks the employee to book a ticket.
:::

He requests an action.

::: examples
- no: Han spør den ansatte om billettprisen.
- en: He asks the employee about the ticket price.
:::

He requests information.

**In an office**

::: examples
- no: Vi ber om en kopi av rapporten.
- en: We ask for a copy of the report.
:::

We want to receive a copy.

::: examples
- no: Vi spør om rapporten.
- en: We ask about the report.
:::

We want information about it.

The noun alone does not decide which expression to use. The speaker’s framing decides: spørre om presents it as a topic, while be om requests an item or action.

## Contrast practice: change the intended result {#contrast-practice-change-intended-result role=contrast objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

For each setting below, write two Norwegian sentences. In the first, request an item or action with be om. In the second, request related information with spørre om.

- a menu in a restaurant
- a ticket at a station
- a room in a hotel
- a document at work

This contrast belongs here because both expressions may occur in the same place and concern the same general topic. A successful pair will clearly change the expected result: something is provided or done in one sentence, while information is supplied in the other.

## Common mistakes {#common-mistakes role=contrast objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

**Using spørre om when you want an item**

Less suitable for the intended meaning:

::: examples
- no: Jeg spør om regningen.
- en: I ask about the bill.
:::

This suggests that you want information about the bill.

If you want the waiter to bring it, use:

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber om regningen.
- en: I ask for the bill.
:::

**Using be om when you want information**

Less suitable for the intended meaning:

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber om åpningstidene.
- en: I request the opening hours.
:::

This may be understood, but it presents the opening hours as something to be supplied rather than a topic you want to ask about.

For a straightforward information request, use:

::: examples
- no: Jeg spør om åpningstidene.
- en: I ask about the opening hours.
:::

**Forgetting the person in an action request**

When you name the person who should act, place that person before om å.

::: examples
- no: Jeg ber resepsjonisten om å ringe en taxi.
- en: I ask the receptionist to call a taxi.
:::

Avoid treating the person as the topic of an information question if your purpose is to request an action.

**Choosing by English translation alone**

English ask can correspond to either Norwegian expression:

::: examples
- no: Hun ber om et håndkle.
- en: She asks for a towel.
- no: Hun spør om håndklærne.
- en: She asks about the towels.
:::

Think about the result, not only the English verb.

## Guided correction activity {#guided-correction-activity role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Rewrite each intended message as a natural Norwegian sentence:

- You want a receptionist to give you a key.
- You want information about breakfast.
- You want a colleague to print a document.
- You want information about a meeting.
- You want a waiter to bring water.
- You want information about the water.

This activity follows the common mistakes because each pair contrasts a requested item or action with information framed as a topic. A successful response will use be om for keys, printing, and water as requested results, while using spørre om for breakfast and the meeting as topics in these prompts.

## Back at reception {#back-at-reception role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Later, Nora’s friend Jonas arrives. Eirik has already booked the taxi. Jonas wants to know the taxi’s exact current price.

::: {.reading translation="Do you know exactly how much the taxi costs?" dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=jonas speaker_name="Jonas" character_id="jonas"}
Vet du nøyaktig hva taxien koster?
:::

::: {.reading translation="No, I do not know exactly how much it costs. Eirik, may I ask about the exact price of the taxi?" dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Nei, jeg vet ikke nøyaktig hva den koster. Eirik, kan jeg spørre om den nøyaktige prisen på taxien?
:::

::: {.reading translation="The price is five hundred and twenty kroner." dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Prisen er fem hundre og tjue kroner.
:::

::: {.reading translation="That’s fine. Then please confirm the booking for me." dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=nora speaker_name="Nora" character_id="nora"}
Det er greit. Da ber jeg deg om å bekrefte bestillingen.
:::

::: {.reading translation="Yes, I’ll confirm the booking now." dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Ja, jeg bekrefter bestillingen nå.
:::

::: {.reading translation="Can we also ask for two cups of coffee while we wait?" dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=jonas speaker_name="Jonas" character_id="jonas"}
Kan vi også be om to kopper kaffe mens vi venter?
:::

::: {.reading translation="Yes, of course." dialogue_id=back-at-reception speaker_id=eirik speaker_name="Eirik" character_id="eirik"}
Ja, selvfølgelig.
:::

Here, the sequence matters. The taxi is already arranged, so Nora first asks for updated price information. After receiving it, she requests the distinct action of confirming the booking. Jonas then requests two items.

## Final contextual practice {#final-contextual-practice role=model objectives=obj-be-om-or-sporre-om-01}

Write a short hotel-reception dialogue with two guests and one receptionist. Include:

- one topic introduction with spørre om immediately before this supplied exchange, using these turns unchanged: Guest — Når begynner frokosten? Receptionist — Frokosten begynner klokka halv sju.
- one request for an item
- one request for a person to perform an action

Keep the situation specific: the guests may also need transport, prices, or hotel services, but every use of be om or spørre om should have a clear intended result.

This activity brings the lesson back to Nora’s setting while requiring independent use of both expressions. Write the spørre om topic introduction and the two be om requests; direct-question construction is not assessed. A successful dialogue will make each framing understandable from context: spørre om introduces breakfast as a topic, while be om requests an item or action.

Without looking back, complete these three tasks:

1. Explain in one sentence how be om frames a requested item or action, while spørre om frames something as a topic.
2. Write one Norwegian sentence requesting an item and one requesting an action.
3. Write one Norwegian sentence using spørre om to ask about a price or opening time as a topic.

Successful review demonstrates the core distinction: be om for an item or action, and spørre om for information.
